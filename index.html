About Ar-Rayyan

Mahaad Ar-Rayyan is an Islamic institution established in 2014 in Kibra, Nairobi, to give girls from poor backgrounds access to quality Islamic education and vocational training.

We are committed to community development, empowering young girls through education and equipping them with the knowledge and skills they need to succeed in life. Our dedicated and experienced team works tirelessly to provide a safe and nurturing environment where every girl can learn, grow and thrive.

Our Vision

To empower young Muslim girls with the knowledge of Islam and practical skills that enable them to lead fulfilling and productive lives, and to become agents of positive change in their communities.

Our Mission

To provide a comprehensive Islamic education and vocational training to young Muslim girls, especially those from underprivileged backgrounds, equipping them to make a positive difference in their own lives and the lives of others.

Our Objectives

  • Provide young Muslim girls with a strong foundation in Islamic knowledge and teachings
  • Offer vocational training programmes that equip them with practical skills
  • Empower young Muslim girls to become agents of change in their communities
  • Foster a safe and nurturing environment in which they can learn and grow
  • Promote social and economic mobility among girls from underprivileged backgrounds, by offering scholarships

Admissions

Who can apply

  • Female applicants aged 14 and above
  • A copy of the birth certificate (or original passport for non-Kenyans)
  • Result slip or school leaving certificate
  • A completed medical check-up form from a health centre
  • Handwritten application letters from parent and applicant
  • Coloured passport-size photos
